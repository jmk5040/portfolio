A looping video work composed from fragments of domestic walls, ceilings, and curtains, scored with field recordings made in the same rooms. The piece runs for forty minutes and never quite repeats.

## Source Material

Filmed across a year in a single rented apartment, using only available light. I never moved the camera; I only waited for the camera to see something.

## Loop Composition

The forty-minute loop is built from twenty-eight short shots arranged in a slowly drifting order. Each playback is technically identical but always feels slightly different — the work is mostly made by the room around the screen.
