A hand-bound artist book pairing found photographs with short, anonymous texts addressed to people the photographer never met. Edition of forty.

## Found Photographs

The photographs were collected over four years from flea markets in three cities. I kept only the images in which someone had clearly been looking carefully at someone else.

## The Letters

Each photograph is paired on the facing page with a short letter — a few sentences, never more than a paragraph — addressed to one of the figures pictured. The letters are written in the second person and signed only with a date.
