A short list, in case it is useful to anyone else.

- Three sheets of cold-pressed paper, taped flat
- A jar of brushes I keep meaning to clean
- One photograph, unfinished, leaning against the window
- A half-translated essay by Yves Bonnefoy, with notes I no longer trust

The light reaches the desk by 3:40 in the afternoon. I am beginning the day at 3:39.

The point of the list is not the list. It is to notice that the same four things have been there since November. I will report back when one of them has moved.
